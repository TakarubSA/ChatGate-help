**Billing** shows your balance, plan and this month's usage in one place.

## Plans and features

Your plan decides which features you have (a lock in the menu marks the ones it doesn't include) and your monthly limits: messages per category, contacts, team members, campaigns and more.

To change plan, pick one under **Plans** and request it. An administrator approves the request before it takes effect.

## Monthly allowance

Messages count against your plan's **monthly allowance** first. The usage bars turn amber at 80% and red when a limit is reached. When the allowance runs out, your plan does one of three things:

| Policy | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Stop | Sending pauses until next month or an upgrade |
| Use credits | Each extra message uses one **message credit** from your balance |
| Bill the extra | Extra messages are added to your next invoice |

## Message credits

Credits are prepaid messages beyond the allowance — **1 credit per message**, used only if your plan allows it. To add credits, pay and then send a **top-up request** with the details; an administrator approves it and the credits appear in your balance.

> [!NOTE]
> **Invoice discount credits** (in SAR) are different: they reduce your next invoice and don't let you send messages.

## Before a campaign

The campaign review step estimates the cost: how many recipients, what the allowance and credits cover, and whether the campaign can go out. If not, it tells you what's missing before you send.

## When something is locked

Opening a feature your plan doesn't include shows a short explanation instead of an error, with the plans that include it.
