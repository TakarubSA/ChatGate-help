The **Inbox** is where every WhatsApp and website conversation lands. The list is on one side, the chat in the middle, and the customer's details on the other side (as a panel on wide screens, or behind the details button on smaller ones).

## Find the right conversation

The tabs at the top of the list cover the usual views:

| Filter | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| All | Every conversation |
| Awaiting agent | Customers who asked for a person and nobody has taken yet |
| Mine | Conversations assigned to you |
| Unassigned | Nobody is assigned yet |
| Active / Closed | Open conversations / resolved or closed ones |
| Pending | Conversations you marked as pending |

**Filters** adds more: high priority, a tag, and the time left in the WhatsApp window. Search finds a customer by name, number or tag, or a conversation by its number.

## The 24-hour window

WhatsApp lets you send free-form messages only within **24 hours of the customer's last message**. The chat header shows the time left.

- While the window is open, write anything: text, files, voice notes, locations.
- Once it closes, you can only send an **approved template**. Pick one from the template button in the composer; the conversation reopens when the customer replies.

> [!TIP]
> Use **Filters → WhatsApp window → Less than 2h left** to catch conversations before they close.

## Write faster

- **Quick replies:** type <kbd>/</kbd> in the message box to insert a ready answer. Manage them in **Settings → Quick replies**.
- **AI assistant:** suggest a reply from the conversation, or improve, shorten, make friendlier or translate your draft (Arabic ⇄ English). Always review before sending.
- **Attachments and voice:** attach files, or record a voice message.
- **Edit and remove:** WhatsApp can't edit or recall a sent message. *Edit* sends the customer a correction; *Remove* hides the message from your team's inbox only.

## Work as a team

- **Transfer** a conversation to a teammate, or take it yourself.
- **Priority** and **tags** help sorting; create tags straight from the details panel.
- **Internal comments** are visible to your team only — mention a teammate to notify them.
- **Notes** on the customer are kept with their profile for anyone who opens their conversations.
- **Close**, **Resolve**, **Mark pending**, **Archive** or **Reopen** from the conversation actions. **Block contact** stops a number from reaching you.

When nobody is free, customers wait in a **queue** (**Settings → Queues**) and are handed to the next available agent.

## Alerts and sounds

You get a card in the corner for new messages, assignments, mentions and hand-off requests, even on another page. Each has its own soft chime. Turn them on or off and listen to each sound in **Settings → Notifications**.

## Export conversations to Excel

Supervisors can download conversations as an Excel file with the spreadsheet button at the top of the inbox list.

1. Choose the **period**: all time, today, the last 7 or 30 days, or a custom range.
2. Narrow it by **status**, **agent** (or unassigned) and **tag** — or tick **Use what the inbox shows now** to export exactly the current search and filters.
3. Keep **Include every message** on to add a sheet with each message (time, sender, type, text, file link, delivery status).
4. Press **Download Excel**.

The file has a *Conversations* sheet (customer, number, status, agent, tags, times, message counts), the optional *Messages* sheet, and a *Notes* sheet with the period. Times are in your workspace time zone.

> [!NOTE]
> A date range keeps conversations that had a message in that period, and only the messages from it. A very large export is capped; the Notes sheet tells you when to narrow the period.
