ChatGate brings your WhatsApp Business number, your team and your automations into one place. Customers write to your number; your team answers from a shared inbox; bots handle the routine questions; and campaigns send approved WhatsApp templates to many customers at once.

## Create your account

1. Open the ChatGate portal and choose **Create account**.
2. Enter your name, your business name, your email, your mobile number and a password.
3. Confirm and sign in.

> [!NOTE]
> One mobile number can belong to one account only. If the number is already used, the form tells you under the field.

The person who creates the account is the workspace **Owner** and can invite the rest of the team later from **Team**.

## Find your way around

The menu on the side is grouped by what you're doing:

| Group | What's inside |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Today's numbers for the whole workspace |
| Conversations | **Inbox**, **Contacts**, **Attributes** |
| Growth | **Campaigns**, **Templates**, **Analytics**, **CTA buttons** |
| Automation | **Bot Builder**, **Workflow Space**, **Integrations** |
| Workspace | **Team**, **Billing**, **Logs**, **Settings** |

A small lock next to an item means your plan doesn't include it yet. Opening it explains what the plan offers and how to upgrade from **Billing**.

On a phone, the bottom bar holds the four pages you use most; everything else is under **More**.

Your photo, language (العربية / English), light or dark theme, the guided tours and **What's new** are in the menu under your name at the top.

## Your first steps

1. **Connect your WhatsApp number** — see [Connect WhatsApp](/en/connect-whatsapp/).
2. **Invite your team** — from **Team**, and pick each person's role.
3. **Set your working hours** — **Settings → General**, so customers get the right reply outside hours.
4. **Write quick replies** for the answers your team sends most — **Settings → Quick replies**.
5. **Create your first template** and send it to yourself as a test — see [Templates](/en/templates/).
6. **Build a bot** for the common questions — see [Bot Builder](/en/bots/).

> [!TIP]
> Every page has a short guided tour. Open the menu under your name and choose **Tour this page** to see it again.

## Language and digits

The portal works in Arabic and English. Switch any time from the menu under your name. Numbers always show as 0-9, and digits typed with an Arabic keyboard (٠١٢…) are turned into 0-9 automatically, so phone numbers and codes are always read correctly.
