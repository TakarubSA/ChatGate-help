title: Team & roles
description: Invite teammates, choose what each one can do, and set up queues and working hours for your support team.

On **Team**, invite a teammate: enter a name and email and choose a role. They receive an email to set their password and join the workspace.

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything, including billing, connections and the team |
| **Supervisor** | Run the inbox and the team: assign, transfer, close, tags, queues, reports |
| **Agent** | Answer conversations assigned to them or waiting in the inbox |
| **Executive** | Read reports and dashboards |
| **Bot Builder** | Build and publish bots |
| **Campaign Creator** | Prepare campaigns — they always go through approval |
| **Campaign Manager** | Send campaigns without approval and approve others' campaigns |

The menu adapts to each person: pages a role can't use don't appear.

## Custom roles

Need a different mix — for example an agent who may also export conversations? **Team → Custom roles** lets you create a permission bundle on top of the fixed roles and give it to a member.

## Queues and capacity

When customers ask for a person and nobody is free, they wait in a **queue** (**Settings → Queues**). For each queue you can set the agents, how many chats an agent handles at once, how long a customer waits before moving to an overflow queue, and the message they receive. A default queue is created automatically the first time it's needed.

## Working hours

In **Settings → General**, set your working hours and holidays (closed dates that override the regular schedule). Outside hours, customers get your out-of-hours reply, and bots can branch on the **business hours check** node.

## Security

- Each person can turn on **two-factor authentication** in **Settings → Security**.
- Workspace-wide rules (for example requiring 2FA) apply to the whole team.
- **Settings → Audit Log** and **Logs** record who did what and when.
