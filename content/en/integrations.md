---
title: Integrations
description: Connect your Salla store, Google Sheets, your own systems through the API and webhooks.
---

**Integrations** connects ChatGate to the tools you already use. What's available depends on your plan.

## Salla

Connect your Salla store to:

- follow up on **abandoned carts** on WhatsApp automatically, and see the recovered sales in Analytics;
- send order and shipping updates;
- use store data in bots (the Salla node) and workflows.

To connect, follow the steps on the Salla card. When asked, register the webhook URL shown there in your Salla Partner dashboard with the same secret.

## Google Sheets

Read from and write to a Google Sheet from bots and workflows — for example, save a lead's answers as a new row, or look up an order status.

## API keys

Under **Settings → API keys**, create keys for your own systems to use the ChatGate API. Give each key a clear name, copy it once (it isn't shown again) and revoke keys you no longer use.

## Webhooks

Bots can call your systems with the **Webhook** and **HTTP request** nodes, and workflows give you a webhook link to start them from outside. Every call in or out is listed under **Logs → API**, with sensitive data masked.
