Every customer who writes to you, or whom you add, is a **contact**. Contacts are what campaigns, bots and workflows work with.

## Add and import contacts

- Add one contact by hand, or start a chat from the inbox with a new number.
- **Import** a CSV or XLSX file — up to 100,000 rows, processed in the background. The first row holds the column names; map each column to a contact field or attribute.

> [!TIP]
> Phone numbers should be in international format (for example `966500000000`). Digits typed in Arabic are converted automatically.

## Lists and segments

| | List | Segment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | A fixed group you add people to | A rule — anyone who matches joins automatically |
| Good for | A one-off audience, an imported file | "Customers in Riyadh who bought last month" |
| Updates | Only when you change it | By itself, as contacts change |

Both can be chosen as the audience of a campaign.

## Held messages

If your plan's contact limit is full, messages from **new** numbers are not lost: they're kept under **Held messages from new numbers** and delivered to the inbox automatically as soon as there's room (for example after an upgrade).

## Bring chat history from your previous platform

Moving to ChatGate from another WhatsApp platform? On the **Contacts** page, **Import chat history from your previous platform** brings each customer's earlier conversations into their conversation here, at the original times. Import your contacts first — the history is matched to them by phone number — then follow the preview before importing.

## Attributes

**Attributes** are the fields you keep about a contact: name, city, order number, last purchase… Some are built in; you can add your own on the **Attributes** page.

They're used to:

- fill a campaign's template variables per recipient (for example `{{1}}` = the customer's name);
- branch a bot or save what the customer answered;
- build segments.
