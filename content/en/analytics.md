**Overview** shows today's key numbers. **Analytics** goes deeper, live, for the period you choose. Every number links to the conversations or campaigns behind it.

## What each tab shows

| Tab | What you learn |
| --- | --- |
| Campaigns | Delivered and read per campaign and per template |
| Agent performance | Each teammate's workload, response time and customer ratings |
| Support KPIs | How fast conversations are answered and closed over time |
| Abandoned cart | With Salla connected: carts followed up on WhatsApp and the sales recovered |
| Click-to-WhatsApp | Customers who came from a Facebook or Instagram ad, per ad |
| Workspace triggers | Workflows triggered from your website, store or API, and the templates they sent |

Above the tabs, the message volume per day and the conversation totals for the period.

A tab with no data yet explains what to do to fill it.

## CTA buttons

**CTA buttons** lists every website and call button across your templates, with results per button: how many messages carried it, how many people clicked, and first-time clickers per day.

Clicks are counted for website buttons that have **click tracking** on (set in the template builder). Each recipient then gets a personal link that records the click before opening your page.

> [!NOTE]
> Click tracking is part of some plans. Without it, website buttons work normally but clicks aren't counted.
